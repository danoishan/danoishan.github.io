A little five-letter challenge

I’m reasonably competitive at Wordle. Here’s a small word game for you: guess a five-letter word in six tries. This is a personal game, not the New York Times game.

After each guess, green means the right place, gold means the letter belongs elsewhere, and grey means it is absent. Screen readers announce each result. No account or personal data is needed.

# Portfolio V3 review guide

This branch is intentionally separate from `main`. It is a content-first portfolio draft, not a résumé redesign.

## What changed

- Editorial / technical-notebook visual direction instead of dark SaaS cards.
- Homepage reduced to positioning, proof, selected work, principles, notes, compressed career context, and personal signal.
- Three long-form case studies with artifacts and ownership boundaries.
- One clearly fictional architecture lab (`Waypoint`) to demonstrate technical reasoning without confidentiality concerns.
- Certifications and employment chronology intentionally de-emphasized.

## Claims to validate before merging

### The 80% Drop
- Reported form submissions dropped by more than 80% after a CRM migration.
- The investigation reasonably grouped issues into capture, consent, identity, attribution, and operating/version complexity.
- The recommended sequencing of reliable capture → customer-data visibility → attribution/reporting accurately reflects the work.
- It is fair to describe your ownership as discovery structure, coordination, roadmap, decision tracking, scope/LOE coordination, client communication, and validation planning.
- The page intentionally does **not** claim a fabricated recovery percentage or revenue result.

### Making 200 Million Messages Boring
- 200M+ annual SMS/MMS and email sends is accurate.
- Requirements, data readiness, QA/UAT, dependencies, budget/burn, resource planning, launch readiness and stakeholder reporting are fair representations of your responsibilities.
- The operating-model artifacts are illustrative reconstructions, not literal confidential client documents.
- No unverified 30% / 20% / forecast-accuracy metrics are used in the case.

### The Form Was Disappearing
- Page JavaScript removing/replacing the form before capture is an accurate root-cause pattern from the real troubleshooting work.
- A CORS allowed-origin issue occurred during validation.
- It is fair to describe your role as structuring the investigation, coordinating owners, clarifying configuration, sequencing retests and closing the loop.
- Production website code and platform changes are explicitly assigned to the system owners rather than claimed as your implementation work.

### Waypoint lab
- Entirely fictional by design.
- The architecture choices should be reviewed for whether they reflect how you would actually advise a customer.
- The page explicitly labels the company, users, scale and data as invented.

## Content direction to review

The intended recruiter impression is:

> Dano has been inside complicated customer-tech programs. He understands the marketing outcome, can follow the architecture and data, works effectively with engineers and specialists, controls project mechanics, and explains ambiguous problems clearly.

If any case feels too technical, too project-management-heavy, or too far from your actual ownership, adjust the *case itself* rather than adding generic résumé content back to the homepage.

03 / How I work

The decision pattern underneath the work.

I do not start with the feature. I start with what has to be true for the customer and the operating team.

01

Start with the business outcome.

Before choosing a platform feature or architecture, I want to know what the customer is trying to achieve, what must be protected and what evidence would count as success.

02

Find the real system boundary.

Then I trace where the fact originates, how the customer is identified and where ownership changes between systems or teams. That is often where the missing assumption or failure lives.

03

Recommend a path and name the trade-off.

Source, identity, latency, scale, failure behavior, operating effort and ownership shape the recommendation. I would rather make a reasoned choice than hide behind five technically possible options.

04

Define proof and failure ownership before launch.

Acceptance criteria, negative tests, monitoring, rollback and an owner for the failure path should exist before go/no-go. A green component is not proof that the customer experience works end to end.

04 / Handoffs, adoption & scale

Launch is a handoff point, not the finish line.

I stay close enough to the work to make sure requirements survive the handoffs, client teams are ready to operate what ships, and post-launch evidence feeds the next decision.

01

Frame

Client and strategy teams align on the business outcome, user need, constraints and success criteria.

02

Translate

I turn the ask into scope, requirements, dependencies, acceptance criteria, priorities and decision context.

03

Build

Design, architecture, engineering, development and data teams receive clear inputs, owners and handoff criteria.

04

Validate

QA, UAT and client stakeholders prove the behaviour end to end rather than validating only individual components.

05

Enable

Release documentation, training, stakeholder briefings, monitoring ownership and support paths prepare the client team to use and operate the change.

06

Optimize & scale

Support issues, customer feedback, adoption signals, operational data and platform analytics become inputs to optimization and broader rollout.

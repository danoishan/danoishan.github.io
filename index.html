03 / How I work

From outcome to operation.

A four-step pattern keeps the work grounded in the customer outcome while preserving clear decisions, handoffs and operating ownership.

01

Frame the outcome.

Align on the business outcome, user need, constraints, what must be protected and the evidence that would count as success.

02

Decide the path.

Trace the system boundary, source, identity, dependencies and failure modes, then recommend a path with the trade-off made explicit.

03

Deliver it safely.

Translate the decision into requirements, owners, specialist handoffs, acceptance criteria, QA / UAT and release evidence.

04

Operate and scale.

Prepare the client team with documentation, monitoring, support and ownership, then use post-launch evidence to optimize and expand the pattern.

200 OK is not the outcome.

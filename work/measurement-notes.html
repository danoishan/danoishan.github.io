Evidence and measurement

What the numbers establish.

Career measures need a definition and an evidence boundary. This page keeps the reported scope, results and public examples distinct.

Individual and team contribution.

My ownership is stated in each case. Production implementation belongs to the named specialist functions, and program results reflect cross-functional delivery. The public portfolio does not isolate individual causal contribution.

Underlying client records remain outside the public repository. Stronger public metric evidence would require an approved, anonymized definition, baseline, comparison period and calculation, rather than an invented reconstruction.

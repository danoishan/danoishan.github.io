When a Billing Journey Goes Quiet

Eight billing journeys were active. If just one stopped sending, the other seven could make overall activity look healthy. I defined a weekly alert for each journey, led testing and release, and checked the monitor in production.

The alert ruleDelivery and testingProduction verification

Count sends for each journey

The client needed a warning they could act on. Each week, the monitor had to look back seven days for each of the eight journeys and name any journey with zero sends. One send in a different journey could never clear the warning.

How I made the requirement testable

I coordinated development and user acceptance testing against the rule, then managed the release. Checking at journey level gave the client a specific place to start investigating when an alert appeared.

What I verified after launch

After launch, I checked that the scheduled monitoring runs were executing in production. I closed the project once I had verified the new check was in service.

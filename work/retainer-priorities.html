When Projects Share One Retainer

I was working on an update to a client’s program blueprint when a new membership journey, billing work and ongoing support all needed time from the same retainer. The client chose to pause the blueprint.

The competing workThe client’s decisionWhat changed later

What could fit in the remaining hours?

In late 2024, we tried to update the blueprint that documented the client’s program. The proposed membership journey alone was estimated in the hundreds of hours. Billing reconciliation, a model for identifying members at risk of leaving, and day-to-day requests drew on the same pool.

On this account I tracked actual hours against the approved retainer, reviewed the demands coming up and reported on capacity in quarterly business reviews. I brought the requirements and timing to Solutions Consulting, who prepared the scope, estimates and budgets with my input.

Why the blueprint paused

The blueprint was worth updating. The question was whether to allocate hours to it while those other project estimates were still in play. The client decided to leave the update on hold.

The priorities changed again

The membership initiative was later put on hold. The billing work also paused, and part of it informed a subsequent rebuild. When I revisited the blueprint decision, I went back to the estimates that were on the table at the time. That explained why a useful update had been deferred even though the competing projects later changed direction.

SAP Cutover Across 20+ Marketing Journeys

An SAP upgrade meant pausing more than 20 Salesforce Marketing Cloud journeys. Customers still needed their transactional messages. I worked with marketing, client IT and our developers to decide which journeys could stop, what would happen to people already waiting, and who could give the all-clear.

What stayed live The pause The restart decision

What had to stay live

I reviewed the active journey versions with marketing and development. We kept critical transactional messages and internal notifications out of the pause list. Getting the version right mattered. Different versions of the same journey could still have customers waiting in them.

The pause window

For journeys set to queue new entries, I checked what would happen to customers who were already in a wait step. The developers kept their existing wait times. That gave marketing a way to explain which messages might still be due when sending resumed.

The developers confirmed the listed journeys were paused inside the agreed window. When the client corrected the version list, I followed up and the developers confirmed those versions were paused too.

When the restart clock could start

The client asked how soon marketing could resume. I gave them a three-to-four-hour restart window starting from the point when client IT confirmed both the SAP migration and the return of the customer preference centre.

That second condition mattered. A customer receiving a marketing email needed a working place to change their preferences. Marketing could use the same all-clear as the developers when planning its next send.

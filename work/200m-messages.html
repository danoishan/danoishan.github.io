SAP Cutover Across 20+ Marketing Journeys

During an SAP upgrade, we needed to pause more than 20 Salesforce Marketing Cloud journeys and keep critical transactional messages running. I coordinated the timing, safeguards, and restart with the client’s IT and marketing teams and our developers.

What could pause?

The SAP cutover window determined when affected marketing journeys could run. An instruction to pause every journey would also affect critical customer messages, so the plan had to identify which communications could stop and which had to stay in service.

I brought IT, marketing and our developers into the same schedule. I tracked the pause and restart timing and kept the transactional exception visible when we discussed what the teams could take offline.

The workback from the upgrade window

The dependency was the upgrade window. My work was to turn that window into actions the Marketing Cloud and client teams could plan around.

What I’d check around the restart

Example verification checks for this type of cutover.

  • Before restart, confirm with IT that SAP is ready and that Marketing Cloud has current inputs.
  • After restart, trace records from the first expected run to the intended step in the journey.
  • Check the protected transactional messages separately. Route discrepancies to the source or platform owner based on where the record stops.

A delayed source feed would change my restart recommendation. I’d bring the delay and the impact on waiting journeys to the client’s release owner.

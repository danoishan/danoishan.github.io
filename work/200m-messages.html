SAP Cutover Across 20+ Marketing Journeys

During an SAP upgrade, more than 20 Salesforce Marketing Cloud journeys needed to pause. I coordinated the pause list with marketing, IT and our developers. Before anyone could restart those journeys, client IT needed to confirm that the migration was complete and the preference centre was back online.

See the cutover workback

What could pause?

Customers still needed critical transactional messages during the upgrade. I worked with marketing and developers to identify the affected journeys and keep transactional messages and internal notifications outside the pause.

The team also needed to know what would happen to people already waiting in a journey and to new records arriving during the pause. Those choices mattered because they determined which steps a customer could reach once sending resumed.

The workback from the upgrade window

For the affected versions configured to queue new entries, the developers kept existing wait times unchanged. I followed up on those settings so the client knew how a customer waiting in a journey would be treated.

What would authorize the restart?

The client asked how quickly we could restart after the SAP all-clear. I gave them a three-to-four-hour window. That let marketing plan for the work still to come after the upstream team finished the migration and restored the preference centre.

For a post-restart check, I’d trace a newly queued record and a customer already in a wait step. They reach the same journey by different paths. I’d verify the protected transactional messages separately.

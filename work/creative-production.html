One Offer, Many Versions

I managed production schedules and approvals across bilingual email, video, motion and social for an automotive client. On one email release, the English partner proof needed a correction just before scheduling. I took the revised file back through testing and sign-off.

The approval recordAcross formatsPartner handoff

The approval had to follow the file

The English and French HTML files went to different distribution partners. The English proof had the wrong alt text on a footer image. Although the change was only a few words, the partner still needed approval on the file they would actually schedule.

I tied feedback to the language, partner and file version. That gave the partner a clear answer about which HTML was ready and gave the client a traceable approval when the English proof changed.

What changed across formats

Elsewhere on the same automotive engagement, vehicle campaigns needed hero videos, shorter cuts and static or motion social placements. I worked with creative on how an approved offer carried across those formats and what each team had to deliver.

Video and motion
Approved claims, cuts, formats and placement-specific exports.
Social
Placement, copy treatment, brand consistency and sign-off for the intended asset.
Bilingual email
Localized copy, subject and preheader, images, links, alt text and language-specific tests.

What I gave the partner

For email handoff, the partner needed the localized HTML, render or seed-test feedback, and confirmation of scheduling clearance. On the corrected English edition, the new partner test was the proof I used to give that clearance.

Since then, when I pass along an approval, I name the file it applies to. The next person should be able to schedule the right version without reopening the whole review thread.

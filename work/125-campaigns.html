Jobs Canada Fair · August 2018 to July 2019

125 Campaigns Across Six Provinces

I led an eight-person team running campaigns and events across six provinces. Each event had a fixed date. Each market had its own partners, audience and details that could throw off the plan.

The national calendarThe workbackScope and results

One calendar, six local plans

Employer outreach, attendee marketing, venue logistics and partner communications ran in parallel. The plan had to be repeatable enough for eight people to manage and flexible enough to work for the next city.

When a partner or local requirement changed, I needed to know which campaign, venue or on-site task would feel it first.

Start with the event date

  1. Work back from the date. Name the partner, campaign and on-site deadlines that can affect each other.
  2. Adapt the outreach. Keep the shared schedule while changing the audience, message and timing by market.
  3. Give each handoff an owner. Keep the local teams and partners clear about who is making each asset or decision.
  4. Review before the next city. Carry forward useful changes from campaign results, team experience and partner feedback.

What we delivered

I owned strategy and execution for more than 125 targeted email campaigns while leading the eight-person team across six provinces.

The wider program reported 10% higher customer retention and 15% higher new customer acquisition. The comparison period and underlying records are private. Read the measurement notes.

I still work back from the launch date and ask who needs what before they can start. At Vigorate the handoff might be a file from a data team. At the fair it might have been a partner approval that affected the local campaign.

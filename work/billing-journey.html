Tracing Failures in Billing Communications

A customer’s bill existed at its source but never reached its Marketing Cloud journey. I worked with the billing provider and platform team to trace the failure. In separate work, I coordinated recovery of missing reports and led a monitoring release across eight journeys.

The missing bill Missing delivery reports Eight billing journeys

A comma changed the shape of the record

The inbound file used commas to separate fields. A comma in a customer’s name made the importer read one extra field and reject the row. Once we compared the source with the actual import, we could explain why the journey never received the bill.

The client agreed to put double quotes around text fields. I also asked the teams to reconcile the affected record before a resend. A second bill would create a different problem for the same customer.

Restoring the reports that had been missed

In a separate incident, the client stopped receiving files that reported bounced billing emails. The question was whether the source data had changed. Our developers found that a reporting step pointed to the wrong extract, while the next step still used a working one.

I coordinated the investigation and followed the missing files through recovery. Once the team fixed the automation, we reprocessed every missed report from the affected period. I confirmed the exports and shared the filenames with the client so their team could reconcile them.

Finding one quiet journey among eight

On a separate monitoring release, the overall send count could look healthy while one billing journey had stopped. I defined a weekly check that counts sends over the previous seven days for each of eight journeys and names any with zero. I led user acceptance testing and release, then verified that the scheduled monitor ran in production.

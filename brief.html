My role at Vigorate

Scope and estimates
I bring the client’s requirements, deadlines and constraints to our Solutions Consulting team. They define the scope and prepare the effort estimate and budget with my input.
Delivery planning
I build the workback schedule, track dependencies and hours against the approved plan, and explain to the client what changes when priorities shift. If we need a revised estimate, I work it through with Solutions Consulting.
Technical decisions
I work with architects and engineers on source fields, identity matching, consent and what happens when a file or event fails. I turn those decisions into requirements and test scenarios that QA and the client can verify.
Release readiness
I track defects, vendor dependencies and approvals against the release window. I bring the evidence and a recommendation to the client’s release owner, including what we can safely defer.
Production and adoption
I review early results with operations, lead follow-up when a release misses the expected behaviour, and use training, reporting and client reviews to plan what changes next.
Scale
20+ concurrent enterprise workstreams per quarter. Programs supporting 200M+ annual email and SMS/MMS sends. Measurement details

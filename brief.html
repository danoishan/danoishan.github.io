My role at Vigorate

Scope and estimates
I bring client requirements, dates and constraints to Solutions Consulting. They define the scope and prepare estimates and budgets with my input.
Delivery planning
I build the workback schedule and track dependencies and hours against the approved plan. When a change affects timing or effort, I bring the options back to the client.
Testing and release
I work with technical teams on decisions about data, consent and integrations. I turn those decisions into tests and bring the evidence and a release recommendation to the client’s decision owner.
After launch
